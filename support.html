How to use Grab2Sheet

  1. Open a normal webpage containing a table, product grid, directory, job listing, property listing, or other repeating structured data.
  2. Open Grab2Sheet from the Chrome toolbar.
  3. Select the detected dataset you want.
  4. Review, rename, reorder, include, or exclude columns.
  5. Export to Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or copy the table to your clipboard.

Common questions

Why does Grab2Sheet not detect the data I want?

Some websites use highly dynamic or unusual page structures. Try rescanning the page. If the field is visible but not detected automatically, use the option to add a field from the page where available.

Does Grab2Sheet upload my extracted data?

No. The current release processes extracted data locally in your browser and does not upload extracted datasets to Grab2Sheet servers.

Does Grab2Sheet work on every website?

No extension can guarantee extraction from every site. Browser-restricted pages, protected pages, unusual virtualized components, and some dynamically rendered interfaces may not be supported.

How do I report a bug?

Email grab2sheetsupport@gmail.com with the website URL, what you expected to extract, what happened instead, and a screenshot if possible. Do not send passwords or private account information.
