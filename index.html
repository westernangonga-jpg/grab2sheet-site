Turn webpage data into clean spreadsheets.

Grab2Sheet detects tables, product cards, job listings, property listings and other repeating webpage data, then lets you preview, clean and export it to Excel or CSV.

Get support

Extract

Detect useful structured data from the page you actively choose.

Clean

Rename, reorder, include or exclude columns and remove duplicate or empty rows.

Export

Download real Excel (.xlsx) or CSV files, or copy the table to your clipboard.

Local-first by design

Grab2Sheet does not require an account. The current release processes extraction, cleanup and export locally in Chrome.

  • No persistent all-sites access.
  • No cloud upload of extracted datasets.
  • No remote executable code.
